# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful,
constructive, and considerate. Disagreement should focus on ideas and evidence,
not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of
private household or credential data are not tolerated.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
necessary to protect contributors or users. Report conduct concerns privately to
a maintainer through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other
project spaces.
