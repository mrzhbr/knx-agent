# Product Naming

## Top Candidates

### MAAT (Recommended)

**Modular Ambient Awareness Technology**

Egyptian goddess of truth, order, and cosmic balance. Keeps everything running correctly.
In German nautical tradition, "der Maat" is the reliable second-in-command who keeps the ship running.

**Why it fits:**
- 4 letters, symmetrical, visually striking, easy to pronounce in any language
- Maat weighed hearts against a feather — judgment, evaluation, sensing what's right
- German "Maat" = competent officer who keeps things running
- Dutch "maat" = measure, standard, companion — all positive associations
- Acronym works: Modular (multi-agent, multi-platform) + Ambient (always present, embodied) + Awareness (perception, state tracking) + Technology
- Clean namespace, no major tech product uses it
- Logo potential: feather of Maat, scales, symmetrical M

**Risks:**
- Obscure Egyptian reference (needs explanation)
- Double-A looks slightly unusual

---

### HestIA (Alternative)

Greek goddess of the hearth — the sacred center of every home.

**Why it fits:**
- "IA" = "AI" in French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese — AI is hidden in the name
- She was the first and last — every gathering began and ended with her
- She chose to stay home while other gods adventured — she IS the house
- Warm, not threatening (unlike ravens, watchmen, all-seeing eyes)
- 6 letters, clean, easy to pronounce

**Risks:**
- Common name for women's shelters and nonprofits
- The AI wordplay only works in romance languages

## Rejected Candidates

| Name | Origin | Why rejected |
|------|--------|-------------|
| HestAI | Greek + AI suffix | Forced -AI suffix, overused pattern, ambiguous pronunciation |
| Argos | Greek (Odysseus's dog) | UK retail brand, the dog dies, too passive |
| Munin | Norse (Odin's raven) | Existing open-source monitoring tool, incomplete without Hugin |
| Aedis | Latin (house/temple) | Sounds like "AIDS" when spoken, pronunciation unclear |
| Domos | Greek (house) | Sounds like Sonos knockoff, generic |
| Heimdall | Norse (bridge guardian) | Marvel owns cultural association, multiple existing tech products |

## Explored Mythological Figures

### Household & Hearth Spirits
- Hestia/Vesta (Greek/Roman) — hearth goddess
- Lares, Penates (Roman) — household guardian spirits
- Genius Loci (Roman) — spirit of a specific place
- Domovoy (Slavic) — household spirit, lives behind the stove
- Tomte/Nisse (Nordic) — household guardian spirit
- Brownie (Scottish) — household spirit that does chores at night
- Zashiki-warashi (Japanese) — child spirit of well-kept houses
- Haltija (Finnish) — guardian spirit of a building
- Vastu Purusha (Hindu) — spirit embedded in every building
- Bes (Egyptian) — fierce household protector

### Wisdom & Intelligence
- Metis (Greek) — wisdom that works from within
- Mimir (Norse) — wisest counselor, keeper of knowledge
- Kvasir (Norse) — born from collective wisdom, could answer any question
- Nabu (Mesopotamian) — god of wisdom and rational arts
- Thoth (Egyptian) — god of knowledge, writing, measurement
- Sia (Egyptian) — personification of perception
- Saga (Norse) — goddess of knowledge and daily counsel

### Awareness & Perception
- Vor (Norse) — nothing can be hidden from her
- Panoptes (Greek) — all-seeing, 100 eyes
- Phanes (Greek) — makes things visible
- Maat (Egyptian) — truth, order, balance

### Threshold & Doorway
- Janus (Roman) — god of doorways, sees inside and outside
- Cardea (Roman) — controls who passes through
- Lamassu (Mesopotamian) — intelligent doorway guardian
